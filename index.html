Beginner Climbing Tips

If you are new to climbing, compare more than just the day pass price. Rental cost, location, and beginner friendliness also matter.

  1. Before you choose a gym
    • Check the day pass price.
    • Check if rentals are included.
    • Look for student discounts or first-time deals.
  2. Before your first visit
    • Fill out the waiver online if possible.
    • Wear comfortable athletic clothes.
    • Bring water and avoid climbing on a full stomach.
  3. Ways to save money
    • Use first-time visitor promotions.
    • Buy your own shoes if you climb often.
    • Consider a membership if you climb every week.
